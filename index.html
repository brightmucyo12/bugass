Got Questions? We’ve Got Answers

How do I turn a website into an app?

Simply enter your website URL, customize your app with your logo, splash screen, and colors, then hit build. No coding or technical skills required. You can test it on a real device before publishing to Google Play or the App Store.

Can I convert my website to app for free?

Yes, Appilix offers a 15-day free trial where you can access core features and test the platform. After the trial ends, the app will still open but prompt users to upgrade.

Can I convert my WordPress website into an app?

Absolutely. Appilix works with any website regardless of how it was built — WordPress, Shopify, Wix, or any custom platform. If your site has a URL, Appilix can turn it into a mobile app.

Can I convert my website to an iOS app?

Yes, and you don't need a Mac or Apple device to do it. Appilix builds the iOS app on its own servers and uploads it directly to App Store Connect on your behalf.

Will my app stay up to date with my site?

Yes, always. Since Appilix loads your website inside the app, any update you make to your website — content, design, or features — is instantly reflected in the app with zero extra work.

Is this just a wrapper?

Appilix does more than wrap your website. On top of displaying your site in a mobile app, it adds powerful native features like push notifications, bottom navigation, navigation drawer, AdMob ads, biometric authentication, and more. You can even control which parts of your website appear in the app using custom CSS and JavaScript.

What is Web to APK?

Web to APK is the process of converting a website into an Android app package (.APK) — the file format used to install apps on Android devices. Instead of building an app from scratch, tools like Appilix take your existing website URL and package it into a fully installable Android app, ready for the Play Store or direct distribution.

Do I need a developer account to publish my app on Google Play or App Store?

Yes, you must have your own active developer account to publish your app — a Google Play Developer account for Android and an Apple Developer account for iOS. Appilix builds the app for you, but the publishing happens under your own account so you retain full ownership.

Ibibazo bikunze kubazwa (FAQ)

Mind bank ni iki?

Mind bank ni urubuga rwabugenewe rwo gufasha abanyeshuri kwitegura neza ikizamini cy'uruhushya rw'agateganyo mu Rwanda binyuze mu myitozo n'ububiko bw'ibibazo.

Mba ngomba kwishyura ifatabuguzi ngo ngere ku bibazo vya Mind bank?

Ushobora gutangirira ku kizamini cy'ibuntu, cyangwa ugahitamo ifatabuguzi ry'umunsi (500 RWF) cyangwa iry'ukwezi (1000 RWF) kugira ngo ubone ibintu byose.

Ni izihe nyungu zo gufata ifatabuguzi mu Mind bank?

Ifatabuguzi ryagufasha kubona ibibazo birenga 300+, gukora ibizamini bitagira umupaka, gukurikirana amanota yawe, n'uburyo bwo kugera mu byiciro byose.
